## Jose Luis Cabrera Vega

**Senior AI Engineer at Scanntech · Statistician (M.Sc.) · Cali, Colombia**

I design LLM systems (RAG, knowledge graphs, agents) and evaluate them the way a statistician would: against a benchmark, with the uncertainty reported, and with a clear line between what was measured and what was assumed.

I came to AI engineering from statistics. Since 2014 I have built predictive and causal models in production settings: crop-yield forecasting with spatial correlation across Latin America, time-series models for a city's environmental monitoring network, churn models and causal impact analysis on hundreds of GBs in Databricks, and knowledge-graph retrieval for LLM applications on Azure.
